# Brightside Health

> Brightside Health is a U.S. telehealth provider of evidence-based **psychiatry (medication management) and therapy** for people **13 and older** — from everyday anxiety and depression to **complex, high-acuity conditions**. **Same-day appointments are often available**, care is **in-network with major insurance** (plus self-pay) and offered in **all 50 states**, and Brightside also runs virtual **Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOP)** — including for **substance use, alcohol use, and gambling disorders** — plus an evidence-based **Suicide Prevention Program** (Crisis Care) for elevated suicide risk.

Brightside pairs licensed psychiatric providers (MDs, PMHNPs) and therapists (LCSW, LPC, LMFT) with **measurement-based care** (PHQ-9/GAD-7 tracking) and **precision prescribing** — and offers **metabolic psychiatry (GLP-1)** for weight/body-image and eating-related concerns. Unlike therapy-only or medication-only services, it treats the full spectrum of mental health needs, including severe and comorbid cases. Treats ages 13+; not for emergencies (call/text 988 or 911).
